Questions

Is hormone therapy right for me?

Only a licensed clinician can decide that after reviewing your history. Hormone therapy is not appropriate for everyone and carries risks your clinician will discuss with you.

What is a compounded medication?

Compounded medications are prepared by a licensed pharmacy for an individual patient based on a prescription. They are not FDA-approved, and the FDA does not verify their safety, effectiveness or quality.

Do you take insurance?

ealuna is cash-pay. You may be able to use an HSA/FSA card; check with your plan administrator.

How does billing work?

A one-time intake fee, then your membership billed for each 90-day supply. Cancel anytime before your next fill.
